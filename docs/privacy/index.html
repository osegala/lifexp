Evrenthia Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Evrenthia ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Evrenthia collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you when you use the Evrenthia mobile application.

1. Information we collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect information such as:

  • Email address
  • User account identifier
  • Display name
  • Authentication information handled through our authentication provider

We do not store your account password directly. Authentication is handled through Amazon Cognito.

App and Progress Information

To provide the core Evrenthia experience, we may store information including:

  • Tasks and goals you create
  • Task completion history
  • Experience points, levels, coins, and streaks
  • Achievements
  • Cosmetic items you own or equip
  • Avatar appearance settings
  • World and building progression
  • Reminder and notification preferences
  • Onboarding status
  • Subscription or entitlement status

Device and Technical Information

We and our service providers may process technical information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Advertising identifiers or similar device identifiers where applicable
  • Diagnostic and request information necessary to operate the application

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Save tasks, progress, avatar customization, achievements, and world progress
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts
  • Provide reminders and notifications
  • Process purchases and subscriptions
  • Provide advertisements to eligible free users
  • Verify rewarded-ad completions and prevent reward abuse
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve the application and user experience

3. Advertising

Evrenthia may display advertisements to users of the free version of the application.

We use Google AdMob to provide advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising identifiers, and information about interactions with advertisements.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be presented with privacy or consent choices before personalized advertising is used.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. If you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement, Evrenthia may verify completion of the advertisement before providing the associated in-app reward.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Evrenthia may offer optional paid subscriptions or purchases.

Purchases are processed through platform providers such as Apple App Store or Google Play.

We may use RevenueCat to manage subscription status and purchase entitlements. RevenueCat may process information such as:

  • App user identifiers
  • Device or platform information
  • Purchase history
  • Transaction information
  • Apple receipts or Google purchase tokens

Evrenthia does not directly receive or store your full payment card information.

5. Cloud services

Evrenthia uses Amazon Web Services (AWS) to operate backend services.

AWS services used by Evrenthia may include:

  • Amazon Cognito for account authentication
  • Amazon DynamoDB for application data
  • AWS Lambda for backend processing
  • Amazon API Gateway for application API requests

Data may be processed and stored using AWS infrastructure in the United States.

6. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Evrenthia may store device notification information required to send reminders and other app notifications.

You can disable notifications through your device settings or applicable settings within Evrenthia.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or process information with service providers only as necessary to operate Evrenthia, including:

  • Amazon Web Services
  • Google AdMob
  • RevenueCat
  • Apple, when using Apple platform purchases
  • Google, when using Google Play purchases or advertising services

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, or to protect the security and integrity of Evrenthia and its users.

8. Data retention

We retain account and application information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Evrenthia, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups or provider systems after deletion where required for security, legal, fraud-prevention, or operational purposes.

9. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your Evrenthia account and associated application data using the account deletion feature within the app.

When an account is deleted, Evrenthia will delete or disassociate account information under our control, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transactional purposes.

Information held independently by third-party providers may be subject to those providers' own retention policies.

10. Your privacy choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Request access to your information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Advertising and consent preferences may also be available through Evrenthia's privacy controls or the consent interface presented by Google.

11. Children's privacy

Evrenthia is not intended for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect user information, including authenticated access and server-side authorization controls.

However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. International users

Evrenthia is operated from the United States. If you use Evrenthia from another country, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will be revised. Material changes may also be communicated through the app where appropriate.
